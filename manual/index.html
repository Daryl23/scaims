SCAIMS User Manual

Smart City Agriculture Information Management System — simulation walkthrough for all three portal roles

Version 7 · 2026

How to read this manual

Each role gets its own set of sheets, two screens per sheet, in the order you use them, drawn as a labelled diagram of the screen — not a photograph. Every diagram carries numbered action markers; the key underneath tells you what to press, in what order, and what to type. All names, numbers and dates are simulation test data.

1 Action marker

Orange circles sit on the exact spot to press. They always run in order: 1 first, then 2.

Amber note

A rule that prevents a rejected record — read it before you submit.

Dotted value

A field you fill in yourself.

Before you sign in

  1. Open the portal address given by the Agriculture Office.
  2. Agency staff and barangay officials press Login as Admin; farmers press Login as Farmer.
  3. Set Language / Wika in the sidebar — it can be changed at any time.

The three roles

Agency Personnel

Sheets 1–2 · English

City-wide monitoring: dashboard totals, crop and damage reports, the analytics behind a monitoring report.

Barangay Officials

Sheets 1–2 · English

The same admin portal, worked one barangay at a time through the barangay filter on every list.

Mga Magsasaka

Sheets 1–3 · Tagalog

Paghahain ng sariling ulat: Home, Sakahan, Ulat sa Inaning Pananim, Ulat sa Nasirang Pananim, Pagpaparehistro, at Aking Impormasyon.

What’s new · Version 7

Version 7 adds farmer validation, land tenure, RSBSA sign-in and stronger account security. The sheets before this one still apply; these are the extra steps for each role. Ang bersyon 7 ay may bagong hakbang para sa bawat tungkulin.

Agency Personnel

English
  1. 1  Validate farmers in User Management › Farmers: press Validate after the in-person check. Only agency accounts can validate.
  2. 2  Set Farmer Status to Active, Inactive or Deceased from the row dropdown. Deceased farmers can no longer sign in.
  3. 3  When staff leave, Deactivate their account under User Management › Admins. Access ends immediately, even mid-session. You cannot deactivate yourself.
  4. 4  Every 90 days you will be asked for a new password: 8+ characters with upper, lower, number and symbol, and different from the current one.

Barangay Officials

English
  1. 1  Search first. If a farmer is not in User Management, guide them through Register on the public page.
  2. 2  You see Validated / Not validated badges but cannot change them — the agency validates after the in-person check.
  3. 3  New Land Tenure column: Landowner, Tenant or Farm Worker. Correct it with Edit if needed.
  4. 4  RSBSA numbers accept the old format (12-34-56-789-012345) and the new one (04-010-14-0123-289AbYQ). One number belongs to one farmer only.

Mga Magsasaka

Tagalog
  1. 1  Mag-login gamit ang Username o RSBSA Number.
  2. 2  Hindi tanda ang RSBSA number? Pindutin ang Hanapin dito sa landing page (RSBSA Finder ng DA).
  3. 3  Sa Pagpaparehistro, piliin ang Land Tenure: Landowner, Tenant o Farm Worker — kasama na ang mga walang sariling lupa.
  4. 4  Sa cellphone, buksan ang Farmer Login at pindutin ang Install Farmer App.
  5. 5  Bagong bersyon ito: kung dati na kayong naka-login, mag-login lamang muli nang isang beses.

The remaining menu items and public pages, so every screen in SCAIMS is covered. Ang iba pang pahina ng sistema.

Agency & Barangay — other menu items

English
  1. 1  Post Updates — type the announcement, attach photos, press Post Update. It appears on every farmer’s Home and on the public page. Delete removes it.
  2. 2  Suggestions — feedback sent from the public page. New ones are listed first; mark them read once handled, and Export Suggestion for meetings.
  3. 3  Crop Management (agency only) — the crop catalog behind every report dropdown. Add Crop with its commodity and display order, then Save Crop.
  4. 4  Satellite Monitoring — choose a parcel to see crop-health (NDVI) history. It stays empty until satellite access is configured; this is a preview feature.
  5. 5  Logout when you leave a shared computer. If your account is deactivated, you are signed out on your next click.

Publikong pahina — walang sign in

Tagalog
  1. 1  Bago magparehistro, sasagutin ang RSBSA Verification: Oo, ako ay nakarehistro para magpatuloy, o Hindi pa para makita ang tagubilin sa pagpaparehistro sa RSBSA.
  2. 2  Tingnan ang Mapa — mapa ng pananim sa Lipa ayon sa uri, barangay at presyo. Hindi ipinapakita ang pangalan ng magsasaka.
  3. 3  Magpadala ng Mungkahi — opsyonal ang pangalan; diretso ito sa Suggestions ng Agriculture Office.
  4. 4  Buksan ang Buong Manwal — ang manwal na ito ay makikita rin sa landing page at sa menu ng bawat account.
