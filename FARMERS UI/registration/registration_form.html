PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 30, 2024

At APGORRS (Agricultural Profile Gateway Online Registration and Reporting System), we prioritize protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled securely. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when using our system.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information from farmers and users of the APGORRS system:
a. Personal Information: Name, address, contact details.
b. Agricultural Information: Farm parcel details, types of crops grown, farm size.

2. How We Use the Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:
a. To register farmers with the Lipa City Agricultural Office.
b. To maintain accurate records of farmer profiles, crops, and farm data.
c. To generate reports for administrative purposes and facilitate decision-making within the Lipa City Office of Agriculture.
d. To send important updates regarding agricultural programs, services, and relevant news.
e. To improve the system's functionality and user experience.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your personal data will only be accessed by authorized personnel of the Lipa City Office of Agriculture. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties, except where required by law or for the purpose of improving services.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. Data encryption, access controls, and regular system audits are part of our security protocol.

5. User Rights

You have the right to:
a. Access the personal data we hold about you.
b. Request corrections to any inaccurate or outdated data.
c. Request the deletion of your data where applicable.
d. To exercise these rights, please contact us at apgorrs@gmail.com.

6. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any significant changes will be posted on our website, and users will be notified.

7. Contact information

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at apgorrs@gmail.com.
