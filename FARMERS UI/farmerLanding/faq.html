APGORRS stands for Agricultural Profile Gateway Online Registration and Reporting System. It is an online platform developed for the Lipa City Agricultural Office to manage farmers' profiles and agricultural data efficiently.
APGORRS is designed for registered farmers in Lipa City, agricultural office administrators, and staff responsible for monitoring agricultural data.
Yes, APGORRS is fully optimized for both desktop and mobile platforms so you can access it from any device.
To register, click the "Register" button on the homepage and fill out the required details including your personal information and farm details.
You will need your basic personal details and specific information about your farm such as location, size, and crop types.

On your dashboard you can: view office updates and announcements, add or update farm parcel information, submit reports to the agriculture office, and track your submission history.
