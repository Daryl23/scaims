About APGORRS

Agricultural Profile Gateway Online Registration and Reporting System (APGORRS) is a specialized platform developed for the monitoring and evaluation of agricultural productivity and output.

It allows for seamless reporting, data collection, and insightful analytics to enhance agricultural performance across Lipa City, Batangas.

  • Seamless farm registration & profile management
  • Real-time agricultural data reporting
  • Insightful analytics for better decision-making
